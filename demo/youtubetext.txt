Marker Spaces is a Paratext tool that finds spacing problems around character markers, footnotes and cross-references, for example a space before a closing marker, or a missing space before an opening one. It shows each problem with the text now and after the fix, and fixes the ones you tick, in the books you may edit. Before changing anything it adds a point to the project history and keeps a backup of each changed book.

Download Install_Phils_tools.exe from the Paratext Plugins folder, and tick Marker Spaces in it:
